# Reference video analysis

Three TikTok screen recordings, analysed frame by frame. Every frame was
extracted (2,249 in total) and measured for motion, coverage and colour; the
tables below describe what is on screen at each beat. Frames between two rows
are the same view in motion (camera turning or a transition in progress).

## What the three videos have in common

1. **The anatomy is a brain, a spinal cord and a torso of nerves.** None of the
   three has arms, legs, hands or feet.
2. **Anatomy is one end of a slider.** The other end is a grouped layout
   (Clusters / Layout). The product is the morph between them.
3. **Grouping is a choice, not a fixed mapping.** A "group by" control decides
   what a cluster is (folder, month, source, type, status, brain function); a
   "layout" control decides its shape (spheres, clusters, rings, ribbon,
   folder tree, treemap).
4. **Every particle is a real note** that can be hovered and opened. Counts are
   700 to about 80,000, not hundreds of thousands.
5. **Particles are crisp and small with little or no bloom.** The background
   is pure black.
6. **The spine and scaffold stay white** whatever the colour mode.
7. **Links are thin coloured polylines**, shown for the hovered or selected
   item, converging on a hub point.
8. **Layout changes are fast direct flights** of about a second, with streaks.

## How the apps decide what goes where in the anatomy

- **Time runs down the spine.** Video B says so outright: "Spine = timeline
  (top = newest)". Video A has "Anatomy order: As it grows"; video C has
  "Order: Oldest first".
- **Each area of life owns a region of the brain.** In video B, selecting one
  area lights one wedge of the brain; colouring by area turns the brain into
  patches.
- **Brain regions can be assigned by function.** Mindcorpus offers
  "Function (anatomy)", which files notes under named regions by the job they
  do.
- **Notes and raw record are separate populations.** Mindcorpus has separate
  Notes and Record toggles, "Notes above · the record below", and a line
  saying a hub was built from record entries "reached beneath the brain". My
  reading, not stated on screen: the brain holds notes and the body holds the
  raw record they came from.

## Where the current build (v5) differs

| | References | v5 |
|---|---|---|
| Figure | brain + spine + torso | full body with arms and legs |
| Meaning of position | time down the spine; area or function in the brain | domain per body band; size and connectivity decide brain / cord / periphery |
| Grouping | switchable (folder, month, source, type, …) | fixed (domain) |
| Layouts | spheres, clusters, rings, ribbon, tree, treemap | clusters and anatomy only |
| Particle count | one per real note | 400k, most of them filler |
| Hover | any speck, shows the note | cluster only |
| Look | crisp, almost no bloom | soft, bloomed |
| Links | polylines for the hovered item | straight lines for the selected unit |
| Spine | always white | coloured by domain |
